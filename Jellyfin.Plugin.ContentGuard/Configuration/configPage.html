Content Guard

Hide titles from specific users by tag, genre or rating. Rules apply on every client and every screen, including home rows, search, Next Up, Continue Watching and direct playback links. Episodes and seasons inherit from their series automatically, and changes apply straight away with no rescan.

Jellyfin 12 options

Search from other plugins, playlist contents and collections are always filtered. The version option needs Jellyfin 12; the others work on every version.
